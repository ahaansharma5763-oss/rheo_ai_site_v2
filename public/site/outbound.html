Six steps. You do the first and the last.

Step 1 · You

You tell us who you want.

The industry, the company size, the cities and the person who signs. One hour with you on how you sell, and we have what we need.

Step 2 · We

We map your market.

We pull the companies that fit from maps, directories, web search and professional networks. An AI agent reads each website and removes the ones that don't fit. We take out every customer of yours that shows in public, and you review our names before we send. Nobody is emailed yet.

Step 3 · We

We hunt for a reason.

We skip the loud news your own sales team already works and look for the quiet signals: a job ad that names their system, a first hire in a new role. Every reason is dated, public and linked to its source, and we check it is still true before we use it.

Step 4 · We

We write to the person who signs.

We find that person, mostly from public pages, and note why it is them. An AI agent drafts the opening line from the source. A person checks the lines: all of them in the first month, a sample after that. Three short plain-text emails, with no tracking and no link in the first one.

Step 5 · We

We handle every reply.

Within minutes each reply is read, sorted and drafted in your voice: interested, a question, not now, a referral, stop. A person approves every answer. "Not now" gets a date, and we come back on that date.

Step 6 · You

You take the call.

When someone says yes, the call lands on your calendar with a brief: who they are, why we wrote and what to ask. Reminders go out, and no-shows get followed up.

Every morning we decide who deserves an email.

Most tools send whatever is next on the list. When follow-ups use up the day's limit, new companies stop hearing from you, and replies dry up soon after. So every morning we count the first emails that will go out, and read the limits back to check they saved.

Will this hurt our company's email?

Your main email is never used. We set up separate web addresses like yours, warm them up and check them every week.

Isn't this just AI spam?

AI does the reading and the first draft. A person checks the lines. Every company is checked before anyone is contacted, every first email opens with a line from a source you can open, and we stop after three emails to anyone who has not replied.

Will you ask for our customer list?

Never. It is the most guarded thing you own. We remove the customers of yours that show in public sources. The only check we ask for is your review of our names.

Where do you get people's details?

Mostly from what companies publish themselves: websites, directories, job posts and news, plus business contact databases where a market needs them. We keep a record of where every contact came from.

How will we know it is working?

We count real people who replied, per person, with auto-replies taken out. Reply rates get quoted per email, per lead and with out-of-office messages included, and the same campaign can look several times better. Ask any agency which one they use.

What if it doesn't work?

60 days after the first email we give you a written review. If the problem is the offer or the market, we say so. We tell you what to change, or recommend you pause.

What do you promise?

We never promise a number of leads, replies or meetings. We commit to the work: your sending set up within 7 days of the day we agree who to reach, and a written review 60 days after the first email.

What does it cost?

We give you one price on the call, once we know your market and you have seen your list.

Three things we will never do.

  • Promise a number of meetings. Only the person we write to decides that.
  • Report how many people opened your email. Apple Mail opens them by itself, so the number flatters everyone.
  • Ask for your customer list. We remove the customers that show in public, and you review our names.

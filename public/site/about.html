Rheo comes from rheō. The Greek word for flow.

Work should flow: enquiries answered, follow-ups sent, nothing forgotten. The drawing on this page is the idea. Thousands of small things moving the right way, without anyone pushing them.

Four rules we keep.

  1. Three emails, then we stop.

    A fourth email is where complaints start to climb. So we never send one.

  2. A line with no source is dropped.

    Every first line comes from something public you can open. If we can't link it, it doesn't go out.

  3. We never ask for your customer list.

    It is the most guarded thing you own. We remove the customers of yours that show in public, and you review our names.

  4. Plain English.

    If you can't explain what we do to your business partner after one call, that is our failure.

Who you deal with.

Ahaan Sharma, founder, takes your first call and reads every request. We work from Pune and answer Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00 UAE time. Write to ahaan@rheoai.co.in, or find Ahaan on LinkedIn.

Three things we will never do.

  • Promise a number of meetings. Only the person we write to decides that.
  • Report how many people opened your email. Apple Mail opens them by itself, so the number flatters everyone.
  • Ask for your customer list. We remove the customers that show in public, and you review our names.

The quickest way to judge us is to see our work.
